# Unified Descent controllability/reward/curriculum probe v1

- Started from `3aa5758`. The candidate bank, XML, action mapping, formal failure definition, frozen π_D and its 1,024,000-count normalizer remained immutable.
- The 16 accepted trust-region updates did move the policy: checkpoint 6400 reached analytic/sample KL `0.00413/0.00634`, deterministic action max/RMS delta `0.00505/0.000971`, and actor relative L2 `2.70e-5`. This movement was measurable but too small to change any candidate's discrete survival result; value prediction RMS moved `0.0192`.
- Small single-channel probes found no mapping/sign/dead-channel fault. The local action Jacobian is full rank from tick 2, but median condition numbers are `419–862` and can exceed `1.6e4`. No isolated channel perturbation added more than one survival tick. Knee is dynamically active but state-dependent and often harmful alone; it must be coordinated with hip, drive and steering.
- Reward-free CEM found physical improvements for most states: `8/14` reached 16 ticks, `3/14` reached 24, median time-to-failure gain was `4.5`, and `9/14` gained at least four ticks. All 14 best sequences replayed exactly. Useful corrections start at tick 0 and are coordinated; hip has the largest median absolute residual.
- Reward alignment passes: 51,200 fixed CEM sequences give candidate-stratified return/survival Spearman `0.7661`, pairwise accuracy `0.8333`, and global Spearman `0.9458`. Action magnitude plus smoothness explain only `0.0077%` of return variance, so they do not suppress the physical correction.
- The unique classification is **C: initialization/exploration gap**. Of the nine sequences with at least four ticks improvement, only one has residual RMS within `2*0.05=0.10`; the others require coordinated corrections outside π_D's ordinary exploration scale. D is therefore not satisfied even though its controllability and reward conditions pass.
