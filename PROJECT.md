# DVGC Project

## Scientific objective

DVGC/JIT studies **iterative real-dynamics jump-capability discovery with
just-in-time curriculum generation** for a fixed single-track two-wheeled robot
model.

The project now distinguishes three levels explicitly:

1. **Physical/task feasibility `F*`** — the unknown set of states from which some
   admissible control behavior could complete the fixed jump task. JIT does not
   prove or exactly compute this set.
2. **Cumulative empirical capability evidence `E_k`** — successful real-dynamics
   evidence accumulated across frozen phase experts and unified policies.
3. **Single-policy realization coverage** — how much of the cumulative support a
   particular unified Actor realizes on a locked evaluation panel.

The final deployable controller is still intended to be **one unified policy**.
The phase experts and frozen intermediate policies are capability probes and data
sources, not a runtime switching architecture.

A Soft Tube is empirical TRAIN support/curriculum guidance derived from
continuation evidence. It is not a certified safe set, viability kernel,
reachability proof, invariant set, or proof of the physical jump limit.

---

## Core JIT idea

JIT couples capability identification and policy learning in a closed loop:

```text
frozen capability probe(s)
        ↓
real-dynamics frontier acquisition
        ↓
continuation evidence near success/failure transition
        ↓
cumulative empirical capability support
        ↓
just-in-time Tube curriculum
        ↓
train one unified policy
        ↓
locked evaluation
  A. did the frontier move?
  B. does the unified policy still realize enough prior support?
        ↓
repeat or open a new method decision
```

The curriculum is not manually prescribed as “easy -> hard.”  It is generated by
the current empirical frontier.

## Iterative phase

For a selected frozen `pi_k` and cumulative Tube support `Tube_k`:

```text
selected pi_k + Tube_k
        ↓
outcome-blind newest-shell frontier plan
        ↓
TRAIN / CALIBRATION / ACCEPTANCE roles
        ↓
real-dynamics frontier acquisition under pi_k
        ↓
pi_k-conditioned continuation labels
        ↓
fit C_up^k / C_down^k on TRAIN
        ↓
calibrate on disjoint CALIBRATION
        ↓
Tube_(k+1)
= all Tube_k retained exactly
+ qualifying TRAIN expansion
        ↓
Tube-RSI smoke + role isolation
        ↓
lock pi_k evaluation baseline before candidate training
        ↓
train/freeze unified pi_(k+1)
        ↓
locked paired evaluation
        ↓
capability-progression analysis
  A. frontier progression
  B. phase-aware policy realization
        ↓
prospectively select pi_(k+1) only when A + B pass
```

This is the future automatic contract.  Historical rounds may contain explicitly
recorded engineering overrides and are not rewritten to look prospective.

## Why zero regression is no longer the envelope definition

The unified policy is reward-guided and currently does not receive an explicit
jump target such as desired distance, apex height, or landing location.
Consequently, the same state may admit multiple reasonable control responses and
a stochastic policy is not expected to reproduce a unique successful rollout for
every previously successful state.

Therefore:

- a single paired regression remains a useful behavioral-retention diagnostic;
- it does **not** erase earlier empirical capability evidence;
- the current policy must instead be judged by phase-aware coverage over the
  cumulative support;
- a severe phase collapse still blocks automatic promotion to sole next-policy
  authority.

Future prospective policy selection uses the capability-progression v1 decision:

```text
frontier progression:
  zero baseline-reproduction mismatch
  nonzero candidate frontier success
  required parent-group diversity
  candidate frontier success in both phases

policy realization:
  global Tube-panel coverage drop <= 5 percentage points
  each phase coverage drop <= 10 percentage points
```

These are method-level engineering non-inferiority margins. They are not a
physical safety or feasibility threshold.

---

## Data-role contract

Development roles remain separated:

- `TRAIN`: may fit `C^k` and may contribute qualifying Tube expansion;
- `CALIBRATION`: continuation threshold calibration only;
- `ACCEPTANCE`: candidate-blind development frontier comparison only;
- final TEST/JCE/JEL: untouched until the method, stopping rule, and final policy
  are frozen.

CALIBRATION and ACCEPTANCE rows never enter a Tube.

Parent-group disjointness is mandatory.  The current Iteration-1 -> pi_2 round
also records an engineering near-observation isolation exception; that historical
exception does not silently relax the future automatic contract.

## Next scientific direction

Do not automatically treat 75/25 -> 90/10 replay as the answer.

The more important representation problem is that the unified policy is not told
what jump behavior is desired.  Before another automatic iteration, the next
method version should consider:

1. **goal-/intent-conditioned unified policy** — one Actor, but with a
   low-dimensional requested jump outcome or behavior code;
2. **multi-seed probability-style policy coverage** — estimate success rates or
   confidence intervals rather than one rollout per state;
3. **discovery-time frozen policy archive** — accumulate system capability
   evidence using older successful probes without runtime expert switching;
   the final deployable controller remains single-policy;
4. only then decide whether a replay-only candidate remains scientifically useful.

No pi_3 work should begin until that next method version is explicitly declared.
